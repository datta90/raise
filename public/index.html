A note before you begin

Your story belongs to you.

Your topics and reflections are saved in this browser on this device so you can return to them. Raise does not create an account or save this app's conversation text on its server.

When you generate an AI plan, the age band, selected options and the context you type are sent to the configured AI provider to create the response. Please do not enter names, school names, addresses, contact details, usernames or other identifying information.

AI suggestions can be imperfect. Edit anything that doesn't sound like you. Raise is not therapy, diagnosis, legal or medical advice, crisis support, or a tool to monitor your teenager.

If anyone may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. For abuse, self-harm or another serious safety concern, involve an appropriate trusted adult or qualified professional rather than relying on Raise alone.
